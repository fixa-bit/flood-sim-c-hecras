Pakistan flood scenario testing — recommended approach
R&D only. Do not implement a custom physics engine. Keep hydraulics, hydrology, and animation as three separate systems, then drive the 3D view from georeferenced model output.

Decision
Build a station-centric scenario lab: any FFD/WAPDA river station (or arbitrary lat/long) snaps to real hydrography, clips FABDEM + soil + rainfall, runs a defendable 2D shallow-water model, and animates those rasters on a WGS84 globe. HEC-RAS 2D is the gold-standard engine. CesiumJS is the viewer. Unreal is optional cinema export only.

Why this split
A cinematic water plane that rises over Cesium terrain is not a flood model. A single 2D SWE run over all of Pakistan at street scale is not computable. Pakistan already operates FEWS, Indus-IFAS/RRI, WRF, and HEC-RAS in FFD; the product should wrap those physics classes, not invent a fourth.

What is defendable
2D shallow-water equations on a documented DEM, Manning n from land cover, Green-Ampt or SCS-CN infiltration from SoilGrids + SMAP, inflows from gauges or HEC-HMS, and skill scores (NSE/KGE) against FFD discharge plus Sentinel-1 flood extent.

What is not defendable
Unity/Unreal water actors, height-extruded Cesium polygons, Google Flood Hub virtual gauges as official truth, or rainfall painted as depth. Those may look better and still be wrong.

Pipeline
Fast screening (LISFLOOD-FP) and gold-standard hydraulics (HEC-RAS 2D) both consume the same hydrology. Animation never computes water.

Scenario model (first-class object)
Simulations are not live weather clones. A scenario is a versioned JSON that names the station, domain, forcings, hydraulics options, and success metrics. The same geometry can replay 2010, 2022, a 100-year monsoon, a Tarbela release, or a bund breach.

Scenario fields
outlet_id / lat-lon, CRS WGS84, DEM source + hash, mesh size, start/end, rainfall product or design storm, soil moisture state, Manning map, inflow hydrograph or rain-on-grid, structures (barrage, levee), output interval, validation gauges.

Scenario library
Historical replay; FFD Low/Medium/High/Very High/Exceptional discharge at the station; return-period rainfall; saturated soil; reservoir release; embankment breach; land-cover change; climate rainfall multiplier. Always store provenance.

Animation that stays scientific
HEC-RAS writes cell water surface, depth, and velocity to HDF5. Rasterize those fields to time-stamped GeoTIFF or RAS Tiles. CesiumJS plays them as TimeDynamicImagery on quantized-mesh terrain built from the same FABDEM clip. Clock, legend, and hydrograph stay locked to model time.

Interactive app
CesiumJS + Cesium World Terrain, overlay local FABDEM, depth colormap, velocity particles, FFD station markers, scenario compare A/B.

Cinema (optional)
Cesium for Unreal only after RAS results exist. Water mesh samples the same depth rasters. Never simulate in Unreal.

Forbidden
ExtrudedHeight flood polygons, fake rain shaders, and unbounded global water planes that ignore the DEM and the hydrograph.

What not to reuse as the core
FFD 3D river-state map is a monitoring product, not an inundation model. Indus-IFAS/RRI is useful as an operational reference and calibration target, not something to reimplement. Google Flood Hub is excellent context (~2,391 virtual gauges) but its virtual series must be labelled estimates. Bentley OpenFlows and MIKE are capable and expensive; they are optional later, not required to start.

Scope guard
First implementation must prove one station end-to-end (recommend Sukkur or Nowshera) with a 2022 historical scenario before opening “any river station of Pakistan” in the UI.
